# Patent pledge

Reroute Holdings LLC holds no patents on Tieroute. We commit never to assert any patent we own or come to own against anyone for implementing the Tieroute specification, in whole or in part, in any software or hardware, commercial or not.

This pledge applies to every version of the specification we publish, and it can't be revoked. It doesn't extend to anyone who sues an implementer of Tieroute for patent infringement over their implementation.
